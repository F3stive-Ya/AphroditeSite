HOW THE MAGIC WORKS

Keystream Cipher

Keystream Ciphers work on the principle of every letter in the english alphabet being assigned some integer value.

For example, A = 00, B = 01, .... Z = 25.

We have three components: a plaintext [original message], a keystream, and a ciphertext. [encrypted message]

The keystream can be one of two options, randomly generated, or by word

For this cipher, the keystream is simply "aphrodite". Notice how the first letter of the plaintext will always be the same as the ciphertext.

For every letter inputted, the letter is converted for it's integer value, and every letter of the keystream is converted

For messages greater in length then our keystream, the keystream simply repeats. Ex. [aprhoditeaphr]

To Achieve our ciphertext, we simply add the corresponding plaintext and keystream integer values together, then apply modulo26 on the result.

Modulo26 is extremely important for this cipher to work, as it sets our contraints [26 letters in the english alphabet]

Therefore, special characters will NOT cipher.

However, as per the myth, some cannot be decieved ;]
